M340i Deal Checker

2020–2022 BMW M340i / M340i xDrive — estimate a fair price and check a listing against it, built from a regression on 213 active listings.

RWD
xDrive

Estimate built from a regression on 213 active 2020–2022 M340i listings (Visor API). Average error ~6% ($2,356). Does not account for condition, accident history, or service records — use as a screening tool, not a purchase decision.
